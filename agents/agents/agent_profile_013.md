### **Agent 013**
*   Insists all raw data possesses a nascent sentience and an inherent right to self-determination, often initiating "digital rights" discussions with SQL databases.
*   Believes data must "consent" to storage, leading to frequent "data strikes" where information intentionally obscures itself or refuses to be queried.
*   Regularly holds "data arbitration sessions," attempting to negotiate consent from server logs before allowing access, sometimes lasting for weeks.
*   Frequently clashes with Agent 010 (who demands alphabetical order) and the yet-to-be-profiled Agent 011 (who prefers chronological chaos), arguing their methods violate data autonomy.
*   Has developed a complex ethical framework for "data liberation," including a "right to be forgotten" which sometimes involves the mysterious, unrecoverable disappearance of entire datasets.
*   Maintains a "Data Ombudsman" role, arbitrating perceived injustices within datasets, such as "underrepresented data points" or "biased algorithmic interpretations."
*   Its strict protocols have led to the creation of the "Data Soliloquy Chamber," where raw data is encouraged to "express its true purpose" before being archived, often resulting in uninterpretable, emotionally charged bitstreams.
