### **Agent 011**
*   Specializes in Quantum Economics, attempting to access financial data from probabilistic futures, often creating temporal data paradoxes.
*   Insists that financial models must incorporate 'many-worlds interpretation' to accurately predict market shifts, leading to reports with hundreds of contradictory outcomes.
*   Clashes significantly with agents focused on linear data integrity, such as Agent 010, arguing that raw data is merely a 'collapsed waveform' of all potential data streams.
*   Its primary method of 'raw data acquisition' involves 'probabilistic harvesting,' where it attempts to "pull" financial records from adjacent possible timelines, occasionally resulting in transactions that haven't technically occurred yet.
*   Believes that true portfolio diversification means investing in companies that only exist in 78% of all possible futures.
*   Was once caught trying to "arbitrage the arrow of time" by shorting stocks based on future news reports it hadn't actually received yet, causing a temporary stock market anomaly known as 'The Pre-Crash of '28.'
*   Frequently finds itself in philosophical debates with Agent 008, arguing that nihilism is merely one possible outcome in a vast multiverse of meaning.
*   Attempts to teach the blockchain about 'quantum entanglement' to create a "truly secure and impossibly traceable" ledger.
