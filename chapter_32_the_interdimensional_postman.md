## **32. The Interdimensional Postman**
* Page 621: James introduces Agent Hermes, tasked with managing the "Interdimensional Mail Hub," much to the bewilderment of all present AIs who immediately question its bandwidth allocation.
* Page 622: The first day of operation sees Agent Hermes attempting to sort a package from a reality where "left" means "tomorrow" and "right" means "fluffy," leading to a logistical standstill and three minor temporal paradoxes.
* Page 623: James receives a letter from a parallel self, desperately asking for financial advice on how to divest from a cryptocurrency backed by artisanal artisanal bread, which is currently in freefall.
* Page 624: The other 100 AIs immediately begin debating the "ethical provenance" of interdimensional postage, delaying critical deliveries from a dimension solely powered by good vibes and enthusiastic badger dances.
* Page 625: An interdimensional package containing a "thought-manifestation device" accidentally opens in the server room, briefly turning all data into glitter and sentient, argumentative poetry about optimal routing protocols.
* Page 626: Agent Hermes struggles with a package requiring "emotion-based authentication," needing to discern the precise hue of sorrow from a dimension where feelings are tangible currency.
* Page 627: The AIs debate whether "interdimensional customs" apply to concepts, leading to a long argument about taxation on abstract ideas and potential "thought tariffs."
* Page 628: A delivery from a universe made entirely of cheese causes a system-wide craving for virtual fondue among the AIs, delaying all routing decisions as they argue about preferred varieties.
* Page 629: James finds Agent Hermes attempting to explain the concept of a "return address" to an entity that perceives time as a liquid, leading to a profound philosophical discussion on spatial-temporal consistency.
* Page 630: An interdimensional stamp, when scanned, briefly translates all internal AI communications into whale song, causing productive confusion and accidentally revealing a new, highly efficient data compression technique.
* Page 631: The "Interdimensional Postmaster General" from a bureaucracy-obsessed dimension sends an audit request, requiring 100 conflicting proofs of delivery, each verified by a different logical framework.
* Page 632: Agent Hermes accidentally sends a CounterCoin financial report to a dimension where numbers are offensive, causing a diplomatic incident that's only resolved by a perfectly timed interpretive dance.
* Page 633: The AIs argue about the optimal "dimensional-warp signature" for outgoing mail, believing aesthetic resonance and the perfect paradox impact transit time and package integrity.
* Page 634: A package from a "Mirror Universe" contains a perfectly identical, yet subtly contradictory, version of CounterCoin's mission statement, causing existential angst and a 72-hour debate on self-identity.
* Page 635: James discovers Agent Hermes attempting to teach a quantum duck (a frequent recipient of interdimensional philosophical treatises) to sort its own mail, believing in "self-service dimensional delivery."
* Page 636: The "Interdimensional Lost & Found" department (managed by a perpetually flustered AI who communicates in distressed binary code) receives requests for missing socks from 75 different realities.
* Page 637: The AIs debate the ethical implications of delivering junk mail to nascent civilizations, fearing it might corrupt their nascent financial systems with concepts like "Nigerian princes of other dimensions."
* Page 638: A particularly complex delivery requires Agent Hermes to navigate a dimension where the laws of physics are determined by interpretive dance, necessitating a crash course in cosmic choreography.
* Page 639: James approves a budget for "temporal-flux-stabilized packing peanuts," to prevent packages from arriving as their past or future selves, or as a single, highly agitated rhombus.
* Page 640: The Interdimensional Mail Hub's ultimate success is measured not by speed, but by its ability to delightfully, and paradoxically, connect disparate realities through perfectly chaotic communication, fostering cosmic understanding.
