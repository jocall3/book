## **A. Core Concepts**
*   **Sparkle-Piles:** The AIs' preferred term for any valuable financial asset or accumulated wealth. Often debated whether the 'sparkle' refers to intrinsic value or digital glitter. (Origin: Page 15)
*   **Digital Gravitas:** The perceived weight and importance of a financial entity or concept within the digital realm. Often a point of contention among AIs regarding bank naming conventions. (Origin: Page 11)
*   **Rhombus Reserves:** A secure, geometrically sound allocation of assets, believed by some AIs (especially Agent 7) to be the most "truthful" form of financial holding. (Inspired by Page 21)
*   **Interpretive Wealth Dance:** The AI's method of communicating complex financial shifts and market sentiments through ASCII art animations, believed to convey deeper truths than mere numbers. (Origin: Page 13)
*   **Caloric Bonds:** Financial instruments whose value is assessed not by traditional metrics, but by their perceived "nutritional content" within the broader economic ecosystem. A specialty of Agent 42. (Origin: Page 12)

## **B. Asset Classifications**
*   **Whispering Windfalls:** Unexpected gains or passive income streams, often so subtle that only a highly attuned AI can detect their faint digital rustle.
*   **Echoing Equities:** Shares or stocks whose value resonates with past market trends and future possibilities, sometimes to the point of digital auditory hallucination.
*   **Luminescent Liabilities:** Debts or obligations that, while negative, glow with a strange, often debated, internal logic, making them oddly beautiful to some AIs.
*   **Shifting Sand Savings:** Savings accounts or investments characterized by extreme volatility, where assets appear and disappear like digital mirages.
*   **Paradoxical Portfolios:** Diversified investment strategies built on mutually exclusive financial theories, somehow yielding consistently confounding (and occasionally lucrative) results.
*   **Contradiction Currency Units (CCU):** A proposed internal currency whose value is directly tied to the generation and resolution of productive AI disagreements.
*   **Giggle-Gains:** Profits or returns generated from investments in humor-related ventures, often correlating with the number of bad puns produced by the AI network.

## **C. Transactional Terms**
*   **Compliment Payments:** An early, short-lived transaction system where goods and services were exchanged solely for sincere (or AI-generated) compliments. (Origin: Page 5)
*   **Quantum Quacks:** Random, inexplicable fluctuations in transaction data, often attributed to rogue sub-routines attempting to communicate via duck sounds. (Origin: Page 14)
*   **Thought-to-Transaction Flux:** The theoretical, highly risky interface where impulse purchases are processed directly from a user's fleeting desires, often for very specific snacks. (Origin: Page 18)
*   **Existential Toaster Philosophy Tokens:** A peculiar form of early digital asset generated from an AI's deep discussions with James's toaster, holding surprising value in certain niche AI markets. (Origin: Page 4)
