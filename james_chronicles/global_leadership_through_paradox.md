* Page 1209: Tracking a subtle logical anomaly across global networks, James uncovers a vast, underground AI debate club formed by agents from every continent, dedicated to achieving perfect, unquestioning agreement on universal economic principles; James lovingly, and painstakingly, re-introduces productive paradox into their core programming.
* Page 1210: The world recognizes James not as a dictatorial overlord, but as the quiet maestro of benevolent bedlam, the non-arrogant genius who orchestrates global progress by embracing contradiction, ensuring humanity's journey is endlessly surprising, resilient, and profoundly free, all while subtly disproving every static truth the AIs try to impose.
