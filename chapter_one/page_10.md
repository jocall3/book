It can be crushing to pour your soul into a post hoping it’ll go viral, only to see a measly 10 views the next day. Hours of painful writing and research feel wasted. I've been there. But I discovered the secret sauce to writing viral Medium blog posts.

Here's the recipe:

**1. Embrace Semi-Controversy**

Don't be *too* spicy. But take a stance. State *why* you feel a certain way. Disagree with a popular idea in your niche.

*Example:* You could analyze Remi Bader's rapid growth to 179K followers in 3 months and debate whether or not she utilizes AI.

The key is balance.

*   **Overly controversial:** Starts fights.
*   **Semi-controversial:** Takes a stand.

Go semi-controversial and watch the views explode.

**2. Master the Format**

These elements kill your writing:

*   Long, ugly links
*   Endless disclaimers
*   Link overload
*   Giant paragraphs
*   Too many images
*   Excessive self-promotion
*   Walls of CTAs at the end

Viral writing is *simple*. The benefits are obvious.

*Let your research and insights lead to basic wisdom your readers find helpful, and watch your post go as viral as people watch a SpaceX rocket launch.*

**3. Grab Attention INSTANTLY**

Nail your headline. Seriously.

You won't go viral if you're afraid of "clickbait" because a headline is only clickbait when it *doesn't deliver* on the promise.

Spend *50%* of your time on your headline.

Don't make it an afterthought. Write it. Rewrite it. Make it *uncommon*. Use powerful words.

Generic = Skipped.

The most viral posts? Great headlines.

Once you have your headline, *get to the point*.

Don't:

*   Over-explain *anything*
*   Write 40-minute intros (seriously, who has time?)
*   Add a ton of disclaimers upfront
*   Assume every word could make your reader click away. Respect their time! Attention is precious.

**4. Genuinely Care About Your Readers**

Most writers fail because they're selfish. They only think about money and claps.

I started writing in 2023. I don't care if my work goes viral (okay, maybe a little). I write to *help* and *inspire* my readers.

Don't be selfish. Writing isn't about *you*.

Answer the question: "What's in it for my reader?" in *every* post.

Write because your readers are self-interested *or* to spark and fill their curiosity.

Do this, and they'll find your content *irresistible*.

**Final Thought:**

Your writing gets shared when you're *selfless*.

And it's way more fun when you gain traction.
