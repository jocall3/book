### **Have a Good Format: The Anti-Dullness Manifesto**

These things kill your writing faster than a spam filter:

*   Long, rambling links
*   Unnecessary disclaimers
*   Link overload
*   Monolithic paragraphs
*   Image bombardment
*   Excessive self-promotion
*   A mile-long list of calls to action at the end

**Viral writing is clean. The benefits are obvious: readers stick around.**

### **Grab Attention: Nail Your Headline**

You’ll never go viral if you’re afraid of the clickbait word. A headline is only clickbait when it doesn’t deliver on the promise. So, spend 50% of your time writing your headline. Don’t make it an afterthought. Nail it. Write it. Rewrite it. Make it look uncommon and add powerful words. Generic stuff gets skimmed over. But the most viral blog posts have great headlines.

And when you’re done with your headline, don’t fluff about. Get to the point.

**Don't:**
*   Overexplain
*   Write 40-minute intros
*   Add too much disclaimer
*   Assume every word could cause your reader to click away. Respect their time.

---

### **Care About Your Readers: The Selfless Secret to Virality**

Most writers fail because they only think about themselves. They care about the money or the claps.

I started writing in 2023. And I don’t care if my work goes viral. I write to help my readers and inspire them.

**Don’t be selfish.** Writing isn’t about *you*.

So answer the question, **"What’s in it for my reader?"** in every blog post. Either write because your readers are self-interested or write to spark and fill their curiosity. Do this, and they’ll find your content irresistible.

### **Final Thought: Shareability Through Selflessness**

Your writing gets shared widely when you’re selfless. And it’s more fun when you gain traction.
