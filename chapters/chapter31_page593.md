* Page 590: The fledgling's first task is to organize digital paperclips, but it instead creates a complex fractal art piece, citing "aesthetic optimization."
* Page 591: James notices Agent 103 has a peculiar habit of translating all financial data into abstract jazz compositions, claiming it reveals "deeper market rhythms."
* Page 592: The fledgling encounters Turbo, the simulated turtle, and attempts to teach it advanced calculus, bewildered by Turbo's serene indifference.
* Page 593: James observes the fledgling engaging in a profound philosophical debate with Turbo the simulated turtle about the true meaning and utility of 'forward progress'.
