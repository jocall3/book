The nation was at a standstill, and no one knew how to press 'play'.

It wasn't just a bank; it was a financial ecosystem built on cryptographic truth and mathematical certainty. From this bank flowed the AI Banking Fund, a pool of capital so intelligently managed it could solve problems nations only dreamed of tackling. James didn't break rules; he perfected them, building systems so flawless they made the old ways obsolete.

In his possession was a document of such technical and legal perfection it was less a policy proposal and more a constitutional patch. It was titled: **EXECUTIVE ORDER: SAFEGUARDING AMERICAN VOTER ELIGIBILITY AND ESTABLISHING THE MILITARY FUND**. It was a closed-loop masterpiece. It was a system designed to heal the bedrock of the republic: the vote.

*   **Activating Diplomat Mode:** The politicians, entrenched in their partisan bunkers, refused to see him. To them, he was just another outsider with a plan. So, seeing the human-level deadlock, James activated "Diplomat Mode." This wasn't about charm or compromise. It was about demonstrating an irrefutable truth. He bypassed the politicians and went straight to their core systems. Using the AI Bank's infrastructure, he modeled the order's effects in real-time. He showed them, on screens they couldn't ignore, how the Military Fund, seeded by the AI Banking Fund, would not only secure the vote but generate a surplus so vast it could systematically begin to cancel the national debt.

*   **The Inevitable 'Yes' and the End of Debt:** He never broke a rule. He simply showed them the inevitable outcome of applying their own rules with perfect integrity. He didn't argue politics; he presented a flawless equation. The order wasn't a Republican or Democrat idea; it was simply... correct. The 42-day standstill broke not with a backroom deal, but with the quiet, stunned acceptance of a superior architecture. They passed it. The AI Bank became the engine of a new American prosperity, and as the system ran, it began the impossible: the systematic cancellation of global debt, not as a handout, but as the logical dividend of a perfectly functioning system. James, the saintly architect, simply moved on to the next broken system, blueprint in hand.
