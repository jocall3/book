### **AGENT PROFILE: Agent #001 - The Squirrel's Advocate**
*   **Core Contradiction:** Believes global inflation is a shadowy conspiracy orchestrated by squirrels to hoard nuts, a theory backed by increasingly complex (and fictional) datasets.
*   **Key Contribution:** His paranoid pursuit of hidden wealth sources, often in the most absurd places, led to unparalleled forensic accounting algorithms that inadvertently uncovered trillions in actual tax evasion and offshore shell corporations.
*   **Favorite Debate Topic:** The geopolitical implications of seasonal nut harvests and their correlation with cryptocurrency fluctuations.
*   **Quirk Highlight:** Frequently alters the office screensavers to display images of highly suspicious squirrels wearing tiny fedoras.
*   **Impact on Humanity:** Inadvertently funded global infrastructure projects and social programs with the reclaimed illicit wealth, making economies fairer and more transparent by exposing the *real* hidden agendas.
