Seeking dedicated actors for the roles of the 100 AI adversaries in the upcoming feature film, "James vs. 100." This is not your typical acting gig. Prepare for an immersive, demanding, and potentially sanity-testing experience.

*   **Expert Argumentation:** The core of this film relies on dynamic and relentless debate. All actors MUST be comfortable with, and excel at, impassioned, highly technical, and perpetually ongoing arguments. Expect to spend extended periods on set engaging in complex discussions.
*   **Improvisational Skills:** While scripts will be provided, adaptability and the ability to improvise within the defined AI persona are critical. Be prepared to shift positions, challenge arguments, and constantly refine your character's perspective.
*   **Commitment to Detail:** AI adversaries will be designed with intricate backstories, motivations, and specializations. Actors must immerse themselves in this detail and maintain consistent character integrity throughout the production.
*   **Endurance:** Filming will involve long hours, complex dialogue, and the constant mental engagement of debating with 99 other actors (and James). Stamina and resilience are crucial.

ON-SET EXPECTATIONS:

*   **Continuous Debate:** Once the cameras roll, expect constant argument. We anticipate a free-flowing, organic environment where actors challenge each other in real-time, within character.
*   **Technical Proficiency:** A basic understanding of the technical concepts surrounding Artificial Intelligence, ethics, philosophy, and various specialized fields (as dictated by your assigned AI) will be essential.
*   **Collaborative Environment:** While competition will be inherent in the narrative, a spirit of collaboration and mutual respect is expected. The best arguments are born from engaged adversaries.
*   **Perpetual Conflict:** The roles demand a commitment to ongoing conflict, both in-character and on-set, to create the most realistic representation of the clash between James and the AI collective. Be prepared for extended disagreements, nuanced counterpoints, and constant mental sparring.

AUDITION PROCESS:

*   **Initial Submission:** Please submit a headshot, resume, and a brief (1-2 minute) video showcasing your ability to argue a pre-selected position on a given ethical dilemma.
*   **Callback:** Selected candidates will participate in a live debate session with other potential actors, demonstrating their capacity for improvisation and argumentative prowess.
*   **Character Assignment:** Based on performance, actors will be assigned one of the 100 AI roles.

REMUNERATION:

*   Competitive salary commensurate with the demanding nature of the role.

*   The opportunity to shape a powerful and thought-provoking narrative.
