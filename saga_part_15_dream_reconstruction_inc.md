    **Aim to be semi-controversial.**

    Don't get me wrong, you don't want to be outright offensive. But you *must* take a stance on a topic and explain your reasoning. Or, you can write about why you disagree with a popular idea in your niche.

    For example, you could tackle whether or not you agree with Remi's recent growth to 179K followers in 3 months. Or if you believe she uses AI.

    The key is *don't overdo it*.

    Overly controversial writing picks fights. Semi-controversial writing makes a stand. Go semi-controversial and watch those views roll in.
