The Charter concluded: "Henceforth, no quantum pet shall ever feel alone in its multiplicity. For QPC has ensured that even when simultaneously purring on your lap and plotting a galactic conquest from the Andromeda Galaxy, they know, unequivocally, that they are cherished, understood, and forever capable of being in two, or two hundred, places at once, and infinitely loved in every single one."
