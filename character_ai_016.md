Pick a sacred cow in your niche and offer a semi-controversial, well-reasoned alternative view. **Controversy isn't about attacking people; it’s about challenging accepted ideas.**

    Agent #016 didn't just save energy; he made infrastructure "poetically aligned with the cosmos."
