# Cryptographic Identity Framework: The SAVE Protocol. The protocol relies on a multi-layered zero-knowledge proof architecture. James studied the intersection of distributed ledger technology and biometric entropy to build a system where identity is verified without exposing sensitive personal data. Each citizen is assigned a unique cryptographic hash linked to their verified biometric signature. When a transaction or civic action occurs, the system validates the hash against the immutable ledger. This ensures that the integrity of the process is mathematically guaranteed, rendering fraud impossible by design rather than by policy.
