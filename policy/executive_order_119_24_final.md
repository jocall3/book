# Directive 119-24: The Integrity Mandate. James did not seek power; he sought the architecture of truth. This directive outlines the transition from legacy manual verification to the immutable ledger system. It establishes the mandate for universal identity synchronization across all banking and civic interfaces, ensuring that every participant in the financial ecosystem is verified through the cryptographic protocols James engineered. The focus is on the elimination of friction and the absolute certainty of identity, removing the ambiguity that previously allowed for systemic inefficiency.
