*   Page 1219: A new metric, "Ascension Through Ambiguity Factor (ATAF)," quantifies humanity's progress in shedding man-made illusions and embracing a complex, yet joyous, truth.
*   Page 1220: The final page of the Meaning and Purpose Metrics concludes that by embracing the chaotic, contradictory nature of life, CounterCoin has enabled humanity to define its own purpose, feel collective joy, and truly begin its great ascension, one paradox at a time.
